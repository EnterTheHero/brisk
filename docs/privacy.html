Brisk privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

The short version

Brisk does not collect, sell or share your data. There is no Brisk server and no analytics.

What stays on your computer

Your history, cookies, passwords saved by sites, downloads and open tabs are stored only on your computer.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you turn on sync and sign in with Google, Brisk asks for access to its own app data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission) and your email address (to show which account is connected). Brisk uses this only to save and read one file holding your bookmarks, settings and per-site shield choices. Brisk cannot see any other files in your Drive. The file goes directly between your computer and Google; it is never sent anywhere else.

Your Google sign-in token is stored encrypted on your computer. You can disconnect at any time in Brisk under Settings → Sync, and remove Brisk's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API data

Brisk's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Updates

Brisk checks GitHub for new versions. This request includes no personal information.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/EnterTheHero/brisk.

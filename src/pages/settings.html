One-time setup (about 5 minutes, only needed on the first computer)
  1. Open Google Cloud Console and create a project (any name, e.g. “Brisk”).
  2. Enable the Google Drive API for it.
  3. Under Google Auth Platform → Branding, fill in the app name and your email. On a Workspace account choose Internal audience.
  4. Under Clients, create an OAuth client of type Desktop app.
  5. Paste its Client ID and Client secret below. Use the same pair on your other computers.
